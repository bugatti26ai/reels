1 good deed. Watch what comes back.

"Whoever comes with a good deed will have ten times the like thereof, and whoever comes with an evil deed will not be recompensed except the like thereof; and they will not be wronged." (Quran 6:160)
مَن جَآءَ بِٱلْحَسَنَةِ فَلَهُۥ عَشْرُ أَمْثَالِهَا

The Prophet ﷺ, narrating from his Lord:
Intend a good deed and don't do it: Allah writes it as a full good deed.
Do it: ten to seven hundred times, to many more times.
Intend a bad deed and don't do it: Allah writes it as a full good deed.
Do it: one bad deed. Only one.
(Sahih al-Bukhari 6491, from Ibn Abbas)

Unfair maths. In your favour.
