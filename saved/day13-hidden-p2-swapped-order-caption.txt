Allah makes this promise twice. The second time, the words are flipped.

"…and do not kill your children out of poverty; We will provide for you and them." (Quran 6:151)
نَّحْنُ نَرْزُقُكُمْ وَإِيَّاهُمْ

"And do not kill your children for fear of poverty. We provide for them and for you." (Quran 17:31)
نَّحْنُ نَرْزُقُهُمْ وَإِيَّاكُمْ

Why the swap?
In 6:151 the parents are already poor (min imlaq). Their worry is their own bread, so Allah says "you" first.
In 17:31 poverty is only feared (khashyata imlaq), the poverty they fear a child will bring. So Allah says "them" first.

Each verse answers the fear in the heart first.

Al-Karmani (Asrar al-Tikrar fi al-Quran) explains the reversal this way: in al-An'am the poverty is already upon the parents; in al-Isra' it is the poverty feared because of the children.
